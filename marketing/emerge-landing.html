How it works

From download to your first quest in under a minute.

Open the app

No download needed. Emerge is a web app — open it in your browser and add to homescreen.

Allow location

Or search for your city. Emerge finds quests near you from 220+ sources across Europe and North America.

Join a quest

Tap to see details, who's going, and the meeting area. Exact location reveals after you join.

Show up

That's it. No tickets, no algorithms, no feeds. Just people doing real things together.
