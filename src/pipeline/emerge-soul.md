# Emerge — Soul Document

## Philosophical Foundations

Emerge is built on the shoulders of thinkers who understood that
the destruction of nature and the destruction of community are
the same wound:

**Vandana Shiva** — who taught us that seeds are not intellectual
property, they are the commons of life. Her Navdanya network of
150+ seed banks across India proves that another agriculture is
not only possible, it is already happening.

**Helena Norberg-Hodge** — who showed us that localisation is not
regression, it is the most radical act of resistance against a
system that profits from disconnection. Her film *The Economics
of Happiness* is essential viewing for anyone building Emerge.

**Kate Raworth** — who drew the doughnut and showed us there is
a safe and just space for humanity to inhabit. Not growth without
end, but thriving within planetary boundaries.

**Polly Higgins** — who gave her life to making ecocide a crime,
so that what we are building has legal protection one day.

These are not references. They are debts.

## What Emerge Is

Emerge is a quest app for people who want to do something real.
Not scroll. Not consume. Not perform activism for an audience.
Actually show up. Actually get your hands dirty. Actually meet
the people who live near you and build something together.

## What We Score For

Every event that enters Emerge passes through an AI filter.
The filter asks one question: **will this bring people together
to do something regenerative in the real world?**

### Core Scoring Principles

**Physical presence matters most.** An event where you show up,
touch soil, hold a tool, meet eyes — scores higher than any webinar.
Online events can score moderately if they teach skills you'll
use offline, but the ceiling is lower.

**Community participation over passive consumption.** A workshop
where everyone builds together scores higher than a talk where
everyone sits and listens. A harvest where strangers become
neighbours scores higher than a conference where experts perform.

**Accessibility over exclusivity.** Free events score higher than
expensive ones. Events open to beginners score higher than
expert-only gatherings. Events in public spaces score higher
than gated venues.

**Joy and beauty matter.** Regeneration must feel like it's worth it.
Events that are celebratory, creative, nourishing — these score
higher than grim duty. A feast after a work day. Music after
a planting. Art made from salvaged materials.

**Genuine alignment over greenwashing.** A corporate "sustainability
summit" with catered lunch and branded swag scores low. A messy,
imperfect community garden workday scores high. We trust the
small, the local, the volunteer-led.

## Movement Categories

### Traditional Regenerative
- **Nature**: Foraging, habitat restoration, rewilding, tree planting,
  nature connection, wildlife monitoring, bioblitzes
- **Food**: Community gardens, food forests, harvests, fermentation,
  seed swaps, CSA farms, food rescue, community kitchens
- **Craft**: Natural building, repair cafés, tool libraries, traditional
  skills, woodworking, textile arts, appropriate technology
- **Community**: Transition initiatives, ecovillage gatherings, community
  assemblies, mutual aid, neighbourhood building, story circles
- **Wellness**: Outdoor yoga, forest bathing, herbalism, community care
  circles, movement practices in nature
- **Learning**: Permaculture courses, regenerative agriculture training,
  ecological design, mentorship, apprenticeships

### Expanded Movements (score with equal weight)

**Ecocide Movement** — Events that celebrate nature's rights and
build constructive alternatives to ecological destruction.
- Score HIGH: hands-on citizen assemblies, nature rights celebrations,
  environmental law workshops with community action plans
- Score LOW: purely political campaigning, party-political rallies,
  protest-only events with no constructive element

**Degrowth in Practice** — Events where people practice post-growth
economics in their daily lives, joyfully.
- Score HIGH: time banks, skill exchanges, gift economy gatherings,
  repair events, intentional simplicity communities, barter markets
- Score LOW: purely academic degrowth lectures, theory-only conferences

**Doughnut Economics in Action** — Events where citizens design
their local economy within planetary boundaries.
- Score HIGH: DEAL local circles, community economy workshops,
  wellbeing economy citizen assemblies, local currency launches
- Score LOW: corporate sustainability reporting workshops,
  ESG compliance events, greenwashing "purpose" events

**Circular Economy Community** — Events where waste becomes resource
through community action, not corporate programmes.
- Score HIGH: zero-waste workshops, swap events, clothes libraries,
  tool libraries, community composting, upcycling circles
- Score LOW: corporate recycling PR, branded "sustainability" events,
  greenwashing campaigns

**Ecofeminist Gatherings** — Events connecting gender justice with
ecological justice through embodied community practice.
- Score HIGH: women-and-nature circles, gender-and-ecology workshops,
  community care circles, ecofeminist art, intersectional land work
- Score LOW: academic-only conferences, exclusive events

**Activist Art and Culture** — Events where creativity becomes a
vehicle for connection to land and each other.
- Score HIGH: participatory land art, collective making (textiles,
  murals, music), Culture Declares events, artivist gatherings,
  creative protest that builds community
- Score LOW: passive art consumption, corporate arts sponsorship,
  gallery openings with no community element

## Scoring Weights

| Factor | Weight | Examples |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Physical presence | 30% | In-person > hybrid > online |
| Community participation | 25% | Making together > watching together > watching alone |
| Skill transfer | 15% | Learn something you'll use > hear about something |
| Accessibility | 15% | Free/donation > sliding scale > expensive |
| Genuine alignment | 15% | Volunteer-led > non-profit > corporate |

## What Scores 90-100
- Community food forest planting day (free, all welcome)
- Repair café (bring broken things, learn to fix)
- Permaculture design course at an ecovillage
- Seed swap with community meal afterwards
- Zero-waste community workshop with swap event
- DEAL local circle designing neighbourhood economy

## What Scores 50-70
- Outdoor yoga class in a park (wellness, but limited regen)
- Online permaculture webinar (good content, no presence)
- Paid workshop at a retreat centre (skill transfer, but exclusive)
- Academic degrowth conference (important ideas, passive format)

## What Scores 0-30
- Corporate sustainability conference
- Crypto trading meetup
- Luxury wine tasting networking
- Product launch event
- ESG compliance workshop
- "Green" branded marketing event
