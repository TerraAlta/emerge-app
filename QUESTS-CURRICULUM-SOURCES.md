## Foundations (Ethics · Principles · Patterns)
- Bill Mollison — *Permaculture: A Designers' Manual* (1988) — the canon; "Pattern Understanding" chapter
- David Holmgren — *Permaculture: Principles & Pathways Beyond Sustainability* (2002) — the 12 principles
- Mollison & Holmgren — *Permaculture One* (1978)
- Toby Hemenway — *Gaia's Garden* — accessible home-scale entry
- Christopher Alexander — *A Pattern Language* — pattern thinking in design
- permacultureprinciples.com (Holmgren) — principle icons + the three ethics
